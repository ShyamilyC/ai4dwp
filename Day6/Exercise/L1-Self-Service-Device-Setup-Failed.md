# My New Computer Won't Set Up

**Version:** v 1.0  
**Date:** 11/08/2026  
**Status:** Draft

If your computer stops during setup or shows an error, your account and company data are safe. This guide walks you through a few simple checks you can do yourself before calling us.

## What you can do now

1. Stay on the welcome or sign-in screen for 10 minutes to allow it to finish.
2. Check that your internet is connected and stable.
3. Restart your computer once.
4. Go through the welcome screens again and sign in with your work account.
5. If it fails again, do not try again — contact the Service Desk.

## Important

- Do not keep restarting many times.
- Do not wipe and start the computer from scratch yourself.
- Do not change or remove the work connection on the computer yourself.

## What to include in your support request

Please share these details so support can fix it faster:

- Your full name
- Your work username or email
- Computer name (shown on a sticker on the computer or on the box it came in)
- Computer serial number (shown on a sticker on the computer or on the box it came in)
- Date and time the issue happened
- Exact error message shown on screen
- A photo of the error screen (if possible)
- Your location and best contact number

Contact the Service Desk if your computer still will not set up after one restart, if the same error appears again, or if you cannot sign in.
