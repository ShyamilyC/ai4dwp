# L1 Self-Service: Device Setup Stuck or Failed

**Version:** v 1.0  
**Date:** 11/08/2026  
**Status:** Draft

If your work device setup stops or shows an error, your account and company data are safe.

## What you can do now

1. Stay on the setup screen for 10 minutes to allow it to finish.
2. Check that your internet is connected and stable.
3. Restart the device once.
4. Start setup again and sign in with your work account.
5. If setup fails again, stop retrying and contact the Service Desk.

## Important

- Do not keep restarting many times.
- Do not factory reset the device yourself.
- Do not remove work account settings yourself.

## What to include in your ticket

Please share these details so support can fix it faster:

- Your full name
- Your work username or email
- Device name (if known)
- Device serial number (if visible on sticker or box)
- Date and time the issue happened
- Exact error message shown on screen
- A photo of the error screen (if possible)
- Your location and best contact number

Contact the Service Desk if setup fails after one restart, if the same error appears again, or if you are unable to sign in.
