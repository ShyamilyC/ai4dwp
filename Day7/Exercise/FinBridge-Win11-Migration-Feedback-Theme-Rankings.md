## Top 2 Themes to Act on Today

### Rank 1 — Credentials Vault Inaccessible (3 comments)

**Why it ranks #1:**
- Highest Blocker volume across all themes (3 users affected)
- Problem has persisted for multiple days — not a one-off incident
- Already escalated to management by at least one user
- Entire teams are blocked, not just individuals
- Without vault access, users cannot do their jobs at all

**Manager summary:**
> "The shared credentials vault has been inaccessible for at least three days across multiple users and teams — this needs immediate escalation and a fix or workaround today."

---

### Rank 2 — Admin Console Lockouts (2 comments)

**Why it ranks #2:**
- Blocker severity — engineers are fully locked out of the admin console
- Pattern is expanding: started with one engineer, now affecting the whole team
- Signals an active, growing incident rather than an isolated fault
- Admin lockouts prevent the team from supporting other users, multiplying the impact

**Manager summary:**
> "Admin console lockouts have spread from a single engineer to the whole team — the blast radius is growing and this needs urgent investigation before it disables our support capability entirely."
