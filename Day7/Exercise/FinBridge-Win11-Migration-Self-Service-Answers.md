# FinBridge Win11 Migration — Self-Service Answers

**Date:** 2026-08-12

---

## Issue 1: Credentials Vault is Not Opening

**Try this first:**

1. Sign out of the vault completely, close the browser or app, then sign back in using your usual work account.
2. If that does not work, try opening the vault in a different browser (e.g. if you normally use Edge, try Chrome).

**Still not working?**
Contact the Service Desk straight away — do not wait. When you call or raise a ticket, please tell them:
- Your name and team
- How many days the vault has been unavailable for you
- Whether your whole team is affected or just you

---

## Issue 2: Locked Out of the Admin Console

**Try this first:**

1. Close the admin console fully and try logging back in. If your password was recently changed, make sure you are using the new one.
2. Check you are connected to the VPN — the admin console will not load without it.

**Still not working?**
Contact the Service Desk and ask them to check your account access. When you get in touch, tell them:
- Your username and which console you are trying to access
- When the lockout started
- Whether any colleagues on your team are seeing the same thing
