## New Theme Identified in Week 2

---

### NEW — Excel Crashes on Large Files
**Week 2 count:** 9 (IDs: 4, 8, 13, 16, 20, 24, 30, 33, 37)
**Severity:** Friction (escalating toward Blocker for Finance)

This theme did not appear in week 1. Excel is crashing or freezing when opening large files (reported as files over 10MB), with users losing unsaved work. The Finance team is disproportionately affected given their use of large budget spreadsheets. With 9 comments in a single week this is the fastest-growing issue in week 2 and needs immediate investigation.

**Representative quotes:**
- *"Excel crash is really disruptive, losing unsaved work each time."* (W2 #13)
- *"Excel crashing has become a real productivity problem for finance team."* (W2 #20)
- *"New Excel crash issue — happens specifically with files over 10MB."* (W2 #33)
