## My account is locked and I can't log in

**Try this first:**
- Wait 15 minutes, then try logging in again — accounts often unlock on their own.
- Make sure the password field is blank before typing; avoid using any saved passwords.

**Call the Service Desk if** you are still locked out after 15 minutes, or this has happened more than once this week. Have ready:
- Your full name and username
- The time it happened
- How many times you have been locked out this week

## My OneDrive files look like they are missing

**Try this first:**
- Check your internet connection is working, then wait 2–3 minutes for OneDrive to finish syncing.
- Look for the OneDrive cloud icon (bottom-right of your screen) — a spinning circle means it is still syncing; give it a few more minutes.

**Call the Service Desk if** files are still missing after 5 minutes. Have ready:
- Your name and which files or folders are missing
- When you last saw them

> Do not move, delete, or recreate any files until IT confirms they can be recovered.

## I can't access the shared S: drive

**Try this first:**
- Sign out of your computer fully and sign back in — this refreshes your connections.
- Open File Explorer and check if the S: drive appears under **This PC**.

**Call the Service Desk if** the drive is still missing or you see a "permission denied" message. Have ready:
- Your name and team/department
- What you see on screen (missing drive or exact error message)
- What work is being blocked

**Service Desk:** 📞 [insert number] | 📧 [insert email]
