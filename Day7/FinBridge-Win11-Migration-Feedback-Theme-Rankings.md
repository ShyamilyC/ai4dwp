# FinBridge Win11 Migration — Post-Migration Feedback Theme Rankings
**Date:** 2026-08-12
**Analyst:** DWP Migration Team
**Source:** 50 end-user feedback comments, post-migration survey

---

## Priority Ranking — Top 3 Themes to Act on Today

Ranking method: severity class first (Blocker > Friction > Minor), then volume as tiebreaker within the same severity class. A single Blocker outranks any number of Minor or Friction comments.

---

### Rank 1 — Account Lockout
**Count:** 5 comments (IDs: 11, 16, 29, 37, 45)
**Severity:** Blocker

**Why it ranks here:** Highest volume among all Blocker-class themes. Five users are completely unable to work, with reports of waiting over 2 hours for IT callback, repeated lockouts across multiple days, and at least one instance occurring immediately before a client call. The pattern across the week suggests a systemic policy or provisioning issue, not isolated incidents.

**Manager summary:** Five FinBridge staff have experienced repeated account lockouts since migration, leaving them entirely unable to work for extended periods, and the lockout policy or authentication configuration appears to need immediate review.

---

### Rank 2 — OneDrive Files Missing or Not Syncing
**Count:** 4 comments (IDs: 14, 23, 34, 42)
**Severity:** Blocker

**Why it ranks here:** Four users report files are missing or not accessible via OneDrive, with explicit urgency in multiple comments (Q1 report due end of day, files needed for a meeting). Data loss perception — even if files are only missynced — is high-risk and erodes user trust in the migration. Ranks above the Shared Drive theme on volume (4 vs 3) at the same severity level.

**Manager summary:** Four users believe critical OneDrive files have been lost following migration, with at least two citing same-day business deadlines, and we need to confirm data integrity and restore access urgently before this becomes a data-loss incident.

---

### Rank 3 — Shared Drive (S:) Inaccessibility
**Count:** 3 comments (IDs: 7, 18, 31)
**Severity:** Blocker

**Why it ranks here:** Three users — including Finance staff — cannot access the S: shared drive at all since migration. One is explicitly blocked from completing month-end reports. The Finance context raises business-criticality above what the raw count alone suggests. Ranks above AVD Login Failure (also Blocker) by volume (3 vs 2).

**Manager summary:** Three Finance staff have had no access to the shared S: drive since migration, directly blocking month-end reporting, and a permissions or drive-mapping regression needs to be investigated and resolved today.
