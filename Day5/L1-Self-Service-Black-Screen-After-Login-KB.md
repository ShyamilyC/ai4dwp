Title: Black Screen After Log In (Steps You Can Do Yourself)
Version: v 1.0
Date: 07/08/2026
Status: Draft

## What this is
If your work screen turns black after you log in, follow these quick steps. This problem is often temporary, and your files are safe.

## Before you start
- Stay calm.
- Do not hold the power button to turn off your laptop unless the IT help team asks you to.
- Your documents and email are not deleted by this issue.

## What you can do safely
1. Wait 30 seconds after you log in.
Some screens recover on their own.

2. Sign out of the work desktop.
Use the sign out button in the app you use to open your work desktop.

3. Wait 1 minute.
This gives your work connection time to reset.

4. Log in again.
Try one new login attempt.

5. If the screen is still black, restart your own laptop.
After restart, log in to your work desktop one more time.

6. If the problem continues, stop trying and contact the IT help team.
Many repeat tries can make support slower.

## Your data is safe
Your files stay in your company account and are not removed by this screen problem. If you were editing a file, anything you already saved is still there.

## Contact the IT help team if...
- The black screen stays longer than 30 seconds.
- You are logged out by itself.
- The problem happens again after one restart.
- You cannot work after the steps above.

## Include these details in your ticket
- Your full name and work login name
- Your location (office or home) and your local time
- Your laptop name (if known)
- Exact time the issue started
- What you saw (for example: black screen, logged out)
- How many times it happened
- What steps you already tried from this article
- A screen picture (screenshot) or photo (if possible)
- Whether this is blocking urgent work
