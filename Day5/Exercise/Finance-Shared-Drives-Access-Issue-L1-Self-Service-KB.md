# Finance Team Folder Access Help

Version: v1.0  
Date: 07/08/2026  
Status: Draft

## What this is
If you cannot open your Finance team folder, do not worry. Your files are safe and still stored in company storage.

## What you can do now
1. Save your open work in other programs.
2. Close any file windows that show the Finance team folder.
3. Sign out of Windows from the Start menu.
4. Wait 60 seconds.
5. Sign back in with your normal work login.
6. Open a file window and check the Finance team folder again.
7. If it opens, continue working as normal.
8. If it still does not open, wait 5 minutes and try once more.

## If the issue is still there
If the Finance team folder still does not open after the second login try, contact the IT help team.

## What to include in your help request
Please copy these details into your help request so the IT team can help faster:
- Your full name
- Your work login name
- Your team (Finance)
- Your computer name (for example: DESKTOP-XXXX)
- Your office or work location (office or home)
- The time the problem started
- The exact message you saw on screen
- Whether you can open other shared folders
- Whether sign out/sign in was tried (yes/no)
- A screen picture of the message (if possible)

## Reassurance
This problem usually affects opening the folder only, not file safety. Your data is not deleted. The IT help team can quickly check this and fix it when needed.

Contact the Service Desk if the Finance team folder still does not open after you sign out, wait, and sign in again twice.
